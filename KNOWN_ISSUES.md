
# Known Issues

This file contains issues that may need further testing or fixing in Christmas Obby.

## Under Investigation

- The progress saving system needs further testing to make sure player progress is saved correctly.
- Checkpoints and respawning should be tested across different stages.
- Coins and leaderboard data should be checked to ensure they update correctly.

## Beta Limitations

- Player progress may be reset during testing or future updates.
- Some stages or gameplay systems may change as development continues.
- Bugs or unexpected behaviour may occur.

## Reporting Issues

If you find a bug while playing Christmas Obby, please report it with:

- A short description of the problem.
- The stage where it happened.
- The steps needed to reproduce it.
- Any screenshots or videos that may help.

## Status

Christmas Obby is currently in Beta. Known issues will be updated as they are discovered, investigated and fixed.
