# Known Issues

This file documents known issues, areas requiring further testing, and current Beta limitations in Christmas Obby.

## Under Investigation

- **Data Persistence:** The new Stages and Coins saving system requires further testing across multiple game sessions.
- **Coins System:** Coin rewards and balance updates should be monitored to ensure consistent behaviour.
- **Checkpoint System:** Checkpoint activation and respawning should be verified across all 200 stages.
- **Player Progress:** Stage progress and respawn locations should remain synchronized after deaths, reconnects, and server changes.
- **Shop System:** Item purchases, Coins deductions, ownership saving, and item availability after respawning require continued verification.
- **Item Equipping:** Speed Coil and Gravity Coil functionality should be tested to ensure they work correctly after purchasing and equipping.

## Beta Limitations

- Player progress, Stages, or Coins may be reset during testing or future updates.
- Some stages, items, and gameplay systems may change as development continues.
- New Shop items may require additional balancing and testing.
- Unexpected bugs or gameplay issues may occur.
- Some visual or interface elements may receive further improvements.

## Reporting Issues

If you find a bug while playing Christmas Obby, please report it with:

- A short description of the problem.
- The stage where it happened.
- The steps needed to reproduce it.
- Any relevant screenshots or videos.
- Information about whether the issue occurred before or after reconnecting.

## Status

Christmas Obby is currently in Beta.

The game features 200 stages, a checkpoint system, Coins, a leaderboard, player data saving, and a functional Shop with purchasable items.

Known issues and potential improvements will be documented and reviewed as development, testing, and new features continue.
